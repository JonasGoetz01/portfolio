The Learning Hub is the system the school runs on. At 42 there is nobody standing
at the front of a room, so the software has to carry what a teacher normally
would: the learning path, the order projects unlock in, and the record of what a
student has actually done.

It grew out of the day-to-day of running the campus. Every feature exists because
something was being tracked by hand, in a spreadsheet, or not at all.
