title: Sommerlager Management Application
subtitle: Registrations, participant data and camp logistics in one place, so the team no longer runs the camp out of spreadsheets.
---

A camp with a few hundred participants generates a surprising amount of
paperwork: registrations, medical notes, group assignments, who sleeps in which
tent. All of it used to live in spreadsheets passed around by email.

The application puts it in one place with proper accounts, so the people who need
a piece of information can find it, and the people who don't, can't.
